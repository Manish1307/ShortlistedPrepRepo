## Before you post

- The jobs, companies and places in the images are made-up samples (Northwind Tech, Contoso Labs, Acme Industries), and the images say "Sample jobs shown". Do not replace them with real company names unless you have permission to use them.
- The website must be live with the Jobs page before you post (deploy the latest version first), otherwise the link will not work.
- "100% Genuine" means every job comes straight from the employer's own career site. A job can still close after it was read, so the Jobs page tells people to check on the company's site. Keep the claim tied to that meaning in comments and replies.

## Tips

- Post on a weekday morning. LinkedIn shows the first two lines, so the hook ("Job hunting is noisy...") carries the post.
- Reply to early comments within the first hour.
- Tag a couple of career coaches or recruiters you know, if they would find it useful.
