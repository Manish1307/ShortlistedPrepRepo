# LinkedIn post: "Tell me about yourself" introduction coach

## Post (copy and paste)

Almost every interview starts with "Tell me about yourself."

It is also the question where candidates unknowingly stretch the truth: a few extra years, a certificate they are "about to finish", a result that was really the team's.

So we built something new at ClassCue: an Introduction Coach that checks your answer against your own resume.

Here is how it works 👇

1️⃣ Answer it your way, out loud or typed, with a timer for the 60 to 90 second sweet spot.
2️⃣ Every fact is checked against your resume and profile: in your resume ✅, only partly ⚠️, or not in your resume ❌.
3️⃣ You see exactly what to change: what worked, what to cut, and the strong results your resume has that you left out.
4️⃣ You get a more impressive version built only from your real facts, plus 6 to 8 personalised bullet points to cover, each tied to the line of your resume it comes from.

Nothing is invented. If it is not in your resume, we will not put it in your mouth.

And it is one step in a complete journey, from resume to offer: ATS-ready resume → AI job search → Skill Gap Matrix and Insight → Apply → Virtual interview coaching → Live interview support. The last image shows the whole path.

If you have an interview coming up, try practising your introduction before the real one.
