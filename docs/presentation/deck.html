The problem

Job seekers lose offers long before the interview

1

Resumes get filtered out

Most large employers use ATS software. A resume that is not written for it is rejected before a person reads it.

2

Job search is noisy

Generic job boards show the same IT roles to everyone. A plant biotechnologist or a teacher scrolls through jobs that are not theirs.

3

Interviews are under-prepared

Candidates do not know their gaps for a role, stretch the truth in "Tell me about yourself", and freeze on follow-up questions.

Today these are solved by separate tools, or not at all: a resume checker here, a job board there, a coach for a fee that many cannot afford.

ShortlistPrep3
Product · AI job search

Relevant jobs for every field, not only IT

  • Reads live openings straight from employers' career sites, so listings are real and current
  • The candidate chooses a sector once; the search then picks the right employers for that field
  • The AI scores every job against the resume and groups them: high, medium and stretch probability
  • Each job gets a Skill Gap Matrix: what matches, what is partial, what is missing, and a plan to close the gap

Why it matters

A candidate in agriculture, healthcare, teaching or finance gets jobs from their own industry. General job boards and many AI tools lean heavily towards technology roles.

Product · Interview coaching

Practice questions made from your own resume

  • Personalised questions built from the candidate's resume, plus scenario questions for the role
  • A model answer and talking points for every question, with a Show answer button
  • Mock interview in the desktop app with spoken or typed answers and a score for each answer
  • The same questions appear in My account and in the support team's dashboard
ShortlistPrep5
Product · New

"Tell me about yourself", checked against the resume

  • Every claim in the candidate's answer is marked: in the resume, only partly, or not in the resume
  • Clear changes: what worked, what to cut, strong results the resume has that were left out
  • A more impressive version and 6 to 8 personalised talking points, built only from real facts

Honest by design. Candidates stop over-claiming, and interviewers see consistent stories.

ShortlistPrep6
Product · Desktop app

Live interview support, with licence and privacy built in

Live Class and interview assistant

A Windows desktop app that listens to a question and shows a suggested answer in real time, fitted to the candidate's topics and resume.

Licence and devices

Signed licences, a limit on computers per licence, trial and paid plans, and activation checked with our server.

Candidate data stays theirs

Resume text is used only to personalise their own answers. AI calls go through our server, so customers never handle API keys.
