Set up your practice interview

Practise for a company, or get questions made only from your own resume. The AI interviewer asks the questions; you answer out loud or by typing, and get a score with feedback after each answer.

The questions come only from the resume you pick. They are saved to your ClassCue account, so you can find them again in My account and your support team can see them.

Practice questions are written by AI in the usual style of each company's interviews. They are not real or leaked questions from the company.

How it works

  1. The interviewer asks a question (read aloud if you like).
  2. Press 🎙 Answer by voice and speak, or type in the box. You can edit the text before sending.
  3. Press Submit answer: you get a score out of 10, what was good, what was missing, and a stronger answer.
  4. At the end you get a report with your overall score and the topics to practise.

Tip: answer out loud even when practising alone. Speaking a clear, structured answer is the skill interviews test.
