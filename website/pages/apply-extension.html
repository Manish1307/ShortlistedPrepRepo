Browser extension

Fill company job forms in one click

Long application forms, dates you have to remember, the same details again and again. The ShortlistPrep Autofill extension fills them from the profile you checked once. You read everything and press Submit yourself.

Install it (testing version)

  1. Get the extension folder from us (the extension folder, or the zip we send you) and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Press Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Open Career toolkit → 6. Apply. It should say Browser extension connected.

A one-click version from the Chrome Web Store will replace these steps at launch.

How to use it

  1. In step 6. Apply, press Fill my profile from my resume, check every job and education entry, then press Save and confirm. Only a profile you confirmed is shared with the extension.
  2. Open a job from your results and press Open the application.
  3. On the company's site a small ShortlistPrep Autofill panel appears. Press Fill this page.
  4. Check what it filled (filled fields get a green outline), fix anything, and continue yourself.

Create account, sign in and later steps

  • Create account: it fills your email and name. It never fills a password: choose a strong one and let your browser's password manager save it.
  • Sign in: it fills your email or username only.
  • After sign-in: portals often move to the next step without reloading. The panel notices the new step and tells you what it found. Tick Fill each new step for me to have it fill every new step automatically.
  • Several jobs and schools: if the form has an Add button for work experience or education, the extension presses it until there is a row for each entry in your profile, then fills every row.

What it never does

  • It never fills a password and never presses Submit, Next or Apply for you.
  • It never overwrites a field that already has something in it.
  • Your profile stays in your browser. It is not sent to the company sites or anywhere else until you press Submit on their form.

Good to know

  • Every company builds its form differently. If a field is not filled, copy it from the Apply kit on the job in your results, or type it in.
  • Dropdowns are filled only when an option clearly matches your profile.
  • Questions that are specific to one company (for example "Why do you want to work here?") and file uploads are left for you.
