What it checks

Contact details, section headings, length, bullet points, action verbs, numbers in your achievements, dates, skills and simple formatting.

How the score works

It is a quick, rules-based check of how well your resume is built for an ATS. It does not compare against a specific job. The full toolkit does that.

Your privacy

Your file is read to score it and is not stored. Only your name, email, mobile number and score are saved.
