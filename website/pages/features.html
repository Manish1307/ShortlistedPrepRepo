Features

Everything you need, nothing you have to manage

{{name}} sits quietly beside your meeting app. Here is what it does for you.

Listening

Hears your class, on your computer

The app captures the sound of your meeting and, if you like, your own microphone. A speech engine that runs entirely on your PC turns it into text. No audio is uploaded or stored.

  • Meeting audio, microphone, or both together
  • Works with the meeting apps you already use (Teams, Google Meet, Skype, Zoom and others), because it listens to your computer's sound
  • Subject hint improves spelling of technical words
  • Live level meter so you know it is hearing

Typical results

Measured on clear, scripted classroom sentences with a topic hint set: about 98% of words correct, at roughly half of real time on a mid-range PC. Noisy or accented real-world audio will score lower. How to test it on your own recording.

Example

You hear: “My question is, what is web services in dot net, and another question is, what is the difference between API and web services?”

{{name}} shows two questions:

  • What is a web service in .NET?
  • What is the difference between an API and a web service?
Understanding

Finds and cleans up the questions

Real speech is messy. {{name}} reads what was just said, finds every question, fixes obvious recognition mistakes, and rewrites each one as a clear question before answering.

  • Several questions in one breath are separated
  • Follow-ups like "what about its derivative?" are made self-contained
  • Greetings and "can you hear me?" are ignored
  • Repeated questions are not answered twice
Answering

Answers that sound like a person

Answers are short and written to be read aloud: the direct answer first, then a plain-language explanation, and an everyday example when it helps.

  • Several questions answered at the same time
  • Pick any question on the left to read its answer on the right
  • Choose Quick, Balanced or Thorough thinking time
  • Choose your AI service and model

A note on accuracy

AI answers can be wrong. {{name}} is an assistant, not an authority: always use your own judgement before telling students something is a fact. Thorough mode can look facts up on the web for extra care.

Control

Built for the way you teach

⌨️

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl Shift N show or hide. L start or stop listening. A answer the last thing heard.

📝

Private notes

A notes tab for answer keys, timing cues and reminders, saved on your computer and hidden from the share.

🔐

Keys stay on your PC

Your AI keys are stored encrypted by Windows and are never shown on screen again.
