Quick start

  1. Install the app and start it. Wait for "Loading the speech model" to finish the first time.
  2. Click ⚙ in the assistant window.
  3. Choose your AI service and paste your API key (see below).
  4. Type your subject, for example "Algebra, Grade 10". The more specific, the better the answers.
  5. Set Audio to listen to to "Meeting + my microphone", choose how a question ends: tick Auto-detect when a question ends (the app decides after you pause), or untick it to press ✔ Question finished yourself, and press Save.
  6. Press Start listening, then teach as usual.
Tip: use headphones. If the meeting plays through speakers, your microphone also picks it up and you may see repeated lines.

Get an AI key

Google Gemini (has a free tier)

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it (it starts with AIza).
  3. Paste it into {{name}} under ⚙ → Google Gemini API key.

School Google accounts sometimes block this page. Use a personal account if so. Free-plan limits apply and prompts on free plans may be used by Google to improve its products.

Groq (free plan, very fast)

  1. Go to console.groq.com/keys and sign in.
  2. Click Create API Key and copy it (it starts with gsk_).
  3. In {{name}}, choose "Groq" as the AI service and paste the key.

Groq runs open models at very high speed, so answers start in about a second. Free-plan limits apply. For the quickest answers in a live class, choose Groq with Thinking time set to Quick.

Anthropic Claude (paid)

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com, sign in and add billing.
  2. Open Settings → API Keys → Create Key, and copy it (it starts with sk-ant-).
  3. In {{name}}, choose "Anthropic Claude" as the AI service and paste the key.

A Claude Pro chat subscription does not include an API key; the API is billed separately.

Over the assistant window the real mouse pointer is always invisible, so it cannot show in a screen share. You still see a pointer: the app draws its own inside the window, and that is left out of the share with the window. Drag the title bar to move the window and the corner mark at the bottom right to resize it.

Other controls: the Export menu saves all questions, answers and response times (text, CSV for Excel, or JSON) into the Documents\ClassCue folder. The assistant window shows no tooltips or pop-up boxes, because Windows draws those as separate windows that a screen share could capture. The percentage box in the title bar makes the window see-through, from 100% down to 10%.

Test that your share stays private

Always do this once before a real class:

  1. Start a meeting with a colleague or a second account of your own.
  2. Share your screen (or the window you plan to share) as you normally would.
  3. Ask them to confirm they can see your content but not the assistant window.

The window is excluded from screen capture using the operating system's feature. Some capture tools, remote-desktop setups and older systems may behave differently.

Check speech accuracy on your own audio

Record a short clip of a real class, write down exactly what was said, and run the built-in test (Windows, in the app folder):

npm run test:stt -- --wav my.wav --ref "the exact words that were spoken" --topic "your subject"

It prints the percentage of words recognised correctly and how fast it ran.

Fixing problems

The assistant is stuck: no answers, or it stopped listening
  1. Click ↻ Restart at the top of the assistant tab. It restarts the sound capture and the speech engine and carries on listening. Your settings, saved keys and question list are kept.
  2. If the window does not react, press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + R.
  3. Still wrong? Open ⚙ Settings, scroll to If something is stuck and click Reset all settings twice. Every setting returns to its default and the window goes back to its starting place. Saved AI keys are kept.
Nothing appears under "Just heard"
  • Check that the level bar moves when you speak. If it does not, check your Windows default microphone and that microphone access is allowed for desktop apps.
  • If you chose "The meeting only", your own voice is not captured. Choose "Meeting + my microphone".
  • Speak a full sentence and pause for a second or two.
It hears the question but no answer comes
  • If Auto-detect when a question ends is unticked, the app waits for you: press ✔ Question finished or Ctrl + Shift + A.
  • Check that an API key is saved for the selected AI service.
  • Read the message on the question card; it says what went wrong.
"Rate limit reached (429)"

You have used up your AI provider's quota for the moment. Wait about a minute, ask fewer questions at once, set Thinking time to Quick, or use a paid key. Free plans have the tightest limits.

"The AI service is having trouble (503)"

The provider is busy. The app retries and tries other models automatically; if it still fails, wait a minute and press the ✔ Question finished button.

The answer is about the wrong thing

Set a more specific subject in the settings, for example "Power Platform: Power Pages, Dataverse Web API". Words with several meanings are resolved using it.

Answers are slow

Set Thinking time to Quick, choose a "Flash-Lite" model, and close other heavy programs. Speech recognition uses your processor, so a slow PC is slower.

Still stuck? Contact support and include your app version ({{version}}) and what you saw.
