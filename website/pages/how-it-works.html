How it works

A practice session, step by step

Ten minutes is enough to feel the difference. Here is what happens.

Choose your role

Pick the job, the level and the type of interview. Add your résumé if you like, so the questions fit your background.

The AI interviewer asks

You hear and read one question at a time, just like a real interview, with follow-ups when your answer needs more detail.

You answer out loud

Speak naturally. The app turns your voice into text so it can understand what you said and how you said it.

You get clear feedback

A score, a structure check, your pace and filler words, and one priority to fix, in friendly, plain words.

Try again, and track it

Repeat the question and watch your score rise. Your progress is saved so you can see the trend over weeks.
