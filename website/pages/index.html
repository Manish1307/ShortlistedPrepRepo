Privacy and visibility

Built to stay out of your screen share

Five things we do, and a two-minute test so you can check them in your own meeting app.

1. Window left out of capture

Windows is asked to exclude the assistant window from screen capture.

2. Its own mouse pointer

The real pointer is invisible over the window. The app draws its own inside it.

3. No tooltips or pop-ups

Hover text and message boxes are separate windows, so none are used.

4. Audio stays on your PC

Speech becomes text on your computer. No recordings are sent or stored.

5. Keys stay encrypted

Your AI keys are encrypted by Windows and never shown again.

How it works

From a spoken question to a private answer

It listens

Your meeting audio, your microphone, or both. Speech becomes text on your computer.

It finds the question

After a short pause, or when you press Question finished. Long scenario questions are kept whole.

It writes the answer

In easy words, using your subject and the earlier answers for follow-ups.

You read it, line by line

One statement per line, in a window your students cannot see.

You keep a record

Export questions, answers and response times when the class ends.
