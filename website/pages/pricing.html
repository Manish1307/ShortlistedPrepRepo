Billing questions

What happens when the trial ends?

The app asks you to enter a licence key. Your notes and settings stay on your computer.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of purchase. See the Refund policy.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account page and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Are taxes included?

Taxes such as VAT or sales tax are added at checkout where they apply.
