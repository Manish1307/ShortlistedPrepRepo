Billing questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Start free with no card, and buy a pass only when you need more.

Does a pass renew automatically?

No. A pass lasts for the days shown and then ends. Buy another one whenever you like; buying the same pass again adds the days to your current one.

How do I pay?

By UPI or bank transfer for now. After paying, enter the payment reference (UTR) and we start your plan as soon as we confirm the payment. Card and net banking payments are coming soon.

Are taxes included?

Prices include GST unless the page says otherwise. You get an order number for every purchase.
