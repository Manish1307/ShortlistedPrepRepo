2. The short version

  • Speech from your meeting and microphone is turned into text on your computer. Audio is not uploaded and is not stored.
  • To write an answer, the app sends the text of a detected question, your subject line and a few recent lines of class text to the AI service you select (Google or Anthropic), using your own key.
  • Your AI keys are stored encrypted on your computer.

3. What stays on your computer

  • Meeting and microphone audio (processed in memory only).
  • Your settings, your private notes, and your AI keys (encrypted with Windows).
  • A local activity log with the text that was heard, the questions detected and any errors. It never leaves your computer unless you choose to send it to us for support, and it is cleared each time the app starts.

4. What is sent to the AI service

When a question is detected, the app sends the text described above to the provider you chose. That provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy, and acts independently of us:

  • Google (Gemini): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Anthropic (Claude): anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Free plans of some providers may use submitted text to improve their products. If that is a concern, use a paid key. Because class speech can include students' words, do not use the app where this is not permitted.

6. Recording and students' privacy

Laws about recording or transcribing conversations differ by country and state, and special rules can apply to children's data (for example COPPA, FERPA, GDPR). You are responsible for telling participants and for having any consent or permission your school, employer, platform or local law requires.

7. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the business (payments, email, hosting), when the law requires it, or with your permission. We do not sell personal information.

Email {{supportEmail}} and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can remove all data held by the app by uninstalling it and deleting its data folder.

9. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. We keep account and billing records as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations.
